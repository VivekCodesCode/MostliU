TWO SIDES OF THE STORY

Rate the place.
Understand the people.

Most PG platforms focus mainly on property listings. EjiPG is designed to add a community layer, so the decision is based on more than photos and rent.

⌂
01 / PROPERTY RATINGS

How is the PG?

Residents can share feedback on the actual living experience.

  • Cleanliness and hygiene
  • Facilities and maintenance
  • Owner or management responsiveness
  • Safety, rules and common areas
◎
02 / COMMUNITY FEEDBACK

Who lives there?

Verified people who share the PG can provide structured, experience-based feedback about fellow residents.

  • Personal and shared-space cleanliness
  • General behaviour and respect
  • Responsibility in common areas
  • Communication and cooperation
HOW EJiPG WORKS

A more informed
move-in decision.

Designed to give prospective residents and PG owners useful context, while keeping feedback tied to real stays and fair processes.

01

Explore a PG

Compare listings and review property-level feedback from people with a verified stay.

02

Understand the community

Where available, view privacy-controlled resident insights that help set expectations about shared living.

03

Contribute after a verified stay

Residents can submit structured ratings based on direct experience, not hearsay or anonymous pile-ons.

04

Make a considered decision

Prospective residents and owners can use relevant information alongside conversations, references and their own judgement.

FOR PG OWNERS

Safer onboarding.
With fairness built in.

EjiPG is intended to help owners understand prospective residents through consent-based profile sharing and verified information — supporting a safer, more transparent onboarding process.

TRUST & PRIVACY

Community insight,
not public shaming.

Ratings about people require stronger safeguards than ratings about a property. Privacy, accuracy and the ability to challenge unfair feedback are core product principles.

Verified participation

Only people with a confirmed connection to the same PG should be eligible to rate a resident.

Controlled visibility

Resident feedback should not be exposed as an unrestricted public profile or searchable public score.

Moderation & appeals

Abusive, discriminatory, retaliatory or irrelevant content should be reportable and reviewable.

Consent & data minimisation

Share only information needed for the stated purpose, with clear retention and access controls.

EjiPG is a product concept under development. Features, verification methods and access rules will be finalised with privacy, security and applicable legal requirements in mind.
